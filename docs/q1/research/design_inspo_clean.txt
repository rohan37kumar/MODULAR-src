DESIGN INSPO
============


PARTS
-----

Saw
  - boomerang blade, short range, returns
  - with Relay, blades bounce through the horde

Hook (trigger)
  - links two guns it touches: one gun's shot carries the other's as a payload released on impact. The rule for which is carrier must be easy to read; show a visible tether. (builder: rule TBD)

Overclock (Chainsaw)
  - touching guns fire 3× faster

Relay (on kill)
  - when a touching gun's shot kills, the gun's shot fires again from the corpse toward the next enemy. Kill chains jump across the horde. Proc decay: each hop is 50% as likely

Mimic (Greek letters)
  - becomes a copy of whatever part type it touches most. A Mimic touching two Mirrors is a Mirror

Fire Cell
  - Ignite
  - shots ignite: 4 dmg/s for 3s. A burning enemy that dies bursts (1-cell radius, 8 dmg) and ignites what the burst hits
  - same
  - [P]


ENEMY
-----

Shielder
  - 40
  - a front shield (a 120° arc) turns to face your Core and blocks every direct hit, beams included (a beam stops at the shield). It does not block area damage (Lobber splash, fire bursts, Sink orb blasts), burning already applied, or anything arriving from behind or the sides
  - rewards non-straight combos: Periscope, Spinner, splash, payloads, Relay hops

23. REFERENCES AND WHAT WE TOOK FROM EACH
-----------------------------------------

Noita
  Taken: spells modify the next spell; order matters; triggers carry payloads; Chainsaw (junk becomes core); Divide's copy cap; Spells To Power
  Done differently: spatial adjacency on a ship instead of a wand list; no self-damage; one thumb

Captain Forever / Reassembly
  Taken: the ship grows from enemy parts
  Done differently: parts stick on contact with no build screen; combos instead of stats

Katamari Damacy
  Taken: things stick to you; the camera zooms out as you grow

Vampire Survivors
  Taken: auto-fire, drag-only, power fantasy
  Done differently: no level-up menu; combos come from rules, not evolution recipes

Binding of Isaac
  Taken: some items replace your shot type (Tammy's Head + Brimstone)
  Done differently: split what fires (guns) from how it's changed (boosters, chainers)

Risk of Rain 2
  Taken: proc chains with decay
  Done differently: Relay's 100/50/25% hops

Breath of the Wild
  Taken: "chemistry engine": verbs act on targets, not on authored pairs
  Done differently: one verb per part, and an interaction table instead of recipes

Ball x Pit / Vampire Survivors evolutions
  Taken: the joy of a named discovery
  Done differently: named combos are recognized, not required recipes

Backpack Battles
  Taken: adjacency synergy
  Done differently: the risk to avoid: combos must change behavior, not just add stats
