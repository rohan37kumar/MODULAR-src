1

The weld

Fly into a floating part and it bonds where it touched. A Mirror has just welded in front of the Beam Lance.

2

The split beam, with its cause on the hull

One branch carries straight on; the other turns 90° at the Mirror. The part that did it is right there on the ship.

3

The link line

A thin line draws through the combo’s parts (Beam → Mirror) for about 1.6 s, so cause and effect are linked on screen.

4

About 0.5 s of slow-motion and a stinger (first time only)

A 40 ms hit-stop, then the game runs at 25% speed for half a second while a stinger sound plays. Nothing extra is drawn: time just slows.

5

The banner: name and quip

The onboard analysis AI names the combo and logs it. Each combo has one quip, written in advance, not generated.

6

The Archive counter

XENO ARCHIVE 1/21 pulses (white, enlarged) as the entry is logged. Discoveries survive the hull failing.

7

The Blueprint unlock

“Blueprint unlocked: Mirror”. The Mirror can now be picked as a starting part next run. This is what makes the next run deliberate.

Repeat firings

Later firings of a combo already in the Archive only flash a small name tag over the ship: no slow-motion, no banner, no stinger.
